Divisions & teams

How divisions work

Seats are teams × players per team. Divisions in the same group (Majors + Minors) share their seats and one registration, and are counted together. Keep teams sends last season's players back to the same team. Records set next season's draft order (“Order by last season's records” puts the worst record first) and show in each kid's history. SportsEngine words match the program a family picks in SportsEngine to this division.

Payment plans — what’s owed

Plans charge the saved card on each due date, retry a declined card every 3 days (3 tries), and email the family a pay link each time. A reminder goes out 3 days before every payment. Click a family for their schedule, contact details, and to email, text or log a call.
