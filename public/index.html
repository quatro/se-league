Divisions

Teams comes from field slots and coaches; seats = teams × roster. SE words are matched against the program a family picks in SportsEngine (- = nobody registers here directly, e.g. Majors). Divisions with the same Group share one registration program and are counted together (Majors + Minors). Keep teams sends last season's players back to the same team. Draft sets up that division's draft room.

Teams & records

Records feed next season's draft order (“Order by last season's records” in a draft room puts the worst record first) and each kid's history.

Paste rosters

Rosters for : a header row with Name (or First / Last), Birthdate or Age, Division, Team. Kids are matched to their existing record by name and birthdate, so each child keeps one history. For a finished season they're recorded as having played, and carried into the next season as returners.
